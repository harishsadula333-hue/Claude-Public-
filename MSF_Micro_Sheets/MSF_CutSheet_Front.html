UI·5Statement: If a,b∈Z, b>0, then ∃ unique integers q,r such that a=bq+r, 0≤r<b.
Proof: Multiples of b: 0,±b,±2b,±3b,…
Case(i) a is a multiple of b ⇒ a=bq (r=0).
Case(ii) a not a multiple of b ⇒ a lies between bq & b(q+1): bq<a<bq+b ⇒ 0<a−bq<b ⇒ 0<r<b where r=a−bq (existence).
Case(iii) Uniqueness: Let a=bq+r and a=bq₁+r₁ ⇒ bq−bq₁=r₁−r ⇒ b(q−q₁)=r₁−r …(1) ⇒ b|(r₁−r). But 0≤r,r₁<b ⇒ |r₁−r|<b ⇒ r₁−r=0 ⇒ r₁=r. Put in (1): b(q−q₁)=0, b≠0 ⇒ q=q₁. Hence proved.
