[UI·1] Q. Define GCD of two integers a and b. | Ans: Let a, b be positive integers. The largest positive integer d which divides both a and b is called the greatest common divisor of a and b, i.e. gcd(a,b)=d. It is also known as Highest Common Factor (HCF). Ex: gcd(8,12)=4.

[UI·5] Q. Define Division algorithm. | Ans: If a, b∈Z and b>0, then there exist unique integers q and r such that a=bq+r, 0≤r<b. q is the quotient and r the remainder. Ex: 17=5×3+2.
[UI·7] Q. Show that √2 is not a rational number. | Ans: Assume √2 is rational: √2=p/q, p,q positive integers, coprime. | Squaring: 2=p²/q² ⇒ p²=2q² ⇒ p² divisible by 2 ⇒ p divisible by 2. Let p=2m ⇒ 4m²=2q² ⇒ q²=2m² ⇒ q² divisible by 2 ⇒ q divisible by 2. | So p and q have common factor 2, contradicting our assumption. ∴ √2 is not a rational number.
