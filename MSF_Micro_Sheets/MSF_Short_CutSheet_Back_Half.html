[UI·10] Q. Write about L.C.M. | Ans: The Least Common Multiple (LCM) of two or more integers is the smallest positive integer that is exactly divisible by all the given integers. | Ex: multiples of 4=4,8,12,16,20…; multiples of 6=6,12,18,24… ∴ LCM(4,6)=12. Also gcd(a,b)·lcm(a,b)=a·b.
[UI·8] Q. Define prime and Coprime numbers. | Ans: Prime numbers: natural numbers greater than 1 having exactly two positive divisors, 1 and itself. Ex: 2, 3, 5, 7. | Coprime numbers: two integers whose GCD is 1. Ex: 8 and 15, because gcd(8,15)=1.
[UI·6] Q. Prove that the greatest common divisor is unique. | Ans: Assume d₁ and d₂ are both GCDs of a and b. Since d₁ is a GCD and d₂ is a common divisor, d₂|d₁. Similarly, since d₂ is a GCD, d₁|d₂. As both are positive integers, d₁|d₂ and d₂|d₁ ⇒ d₁=d₂. Therefore the GCD is unique. Hence proved.
[UI·4] Q. State Fundamental theorem of arithmetic. | Ans: Every positive integer n>1 can be expressed as a product of primes uniquely (except for order), i.e. n=p₁^α₁·p₂^α₂·…·p_k^α_k where p₁<p₂<…<p_k are distinct primes dividing n. Ex: 60=2²×3×5.
[UI·2] Q. The sum of two odd integers is an even integer. | Ans: Proof: Let a, b be two odd integers: a=2n₁+1, b=2n₂+1, n₁,n₂∈Z. | a+b=2n₁+1+2n₂+1=2n₁+2n₂+2=2(n₁+n₂+1)=2n₃ [n₃=n₁+n₂+1∈Z]. | ∴ a+b is an even integer.

[UII·6] Q. Mean=np, Variance=npq. | Examples: 1) No. of defective bolts in a box of n bolts. 2) No. of post-graduates in a group of n men. 3) No. of heads in n coin tosses.

[UIV·10] Q. Explain the terms Null hypothesis and Alternative hypothesis. | Ans: (Not in answer sheet; standard definitions) | Null hypothesis (H₀): a hypothesis of no difference, tested for possible rejection under the assumption that it is true. Ex: H₀: μ=μ₀. | Alternative hypothesis (H₁): any hypothesis complementary to H₀, accepted when H₀ is rejected. Ex: H₁: μ≠μ₀ (two-tailed), μ>μ₀ or μ<μ₀ (one-tailed).
[UIV·8] Q. Write about the test statistic of difference of proportion. | Ans: H₀: P₁=P₂. z=(p₁−p₂)/S.E.(p₁−p₂). | (a) P₁,P₂ known: z=(p₁−p₂)/√(P₁Q₁/n₁+P₂Q₂/n₂). | (b) Unknown: (i) Substitution: z=(p₁−p₂)/√(p₁q₁/n₁+p₂q₂/n₂). (ii) Pooling: p=(n₁p₁+n₂p₂)/(n₁+n₂), q=1−p, z=(p₁−p₂)/√[pq(1/n₁+1/n₂)]. | Reject H₀ if |z|>1.96 (5%), >2.58 (1%), >1.645 (10%).
[UIV·6] Q. Write the procedure for Hypothesis testing. | Ans: 1) State the null hypothesis (H₀) and alternative hypothesis (H₁). | 2) Choose the significance level (α), usually 0.05. | 3) Select the suitable statistical test (z-test, t-test etc.). | 4) Calculate the test statistic using sample data. | 5) Find the critical value or p-value. | 6) Make a decision: reject H₀ if |z|>critical value, else accept. | 7) Write the conclusion in simple terms.
[UIV·4] Q. What is a Type I error? | Ans: A Type I error occurs when the null hypothesis is rejected when it is actually true. Its probability is the level of significance α. (Type II error: accepting H₀ when it is false.)
[UIV·2] Q. State interval of estimation. | Ans: Interval of estimation is a range of values used to estimate an unknown population parameter (like mean or proportion) instead of giving a single value. Ex: 95% CI for μ: (x̄−1.96σ/√n, x̄+1.96σ/√n).
