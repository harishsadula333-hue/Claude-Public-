Subject: Proposal for a collaborative Advent Calendar Creation Workshop

Dear Team,

Sundarlysh Memories is pleased to propose a creative workshop partnership with Aryka Studio for a festive, hands-on experience in Hyderabad. The collaboration brings together Sundarlysh Memories' workshop concept and creative execution with Aryka Studio's space, hospitality and community reach. Our shared aim is to create an engaging event that offers value and visibility to both partners.

1. Workshop overview

WorkshopCreate Your Own Advent Calendar
DateSunday, 29 November 2026
Time5:00 PM – 7:00 PM
Collaboration partnerAryka Studio
Workshop concept & executionSundarlysh Memories
Participant ticket₹1,199 per person

2. The workshop concept

We plan to introduce Hyderabad to a unique, first-time Advent Calendar Creation Workshop. Participants will receive a thoughtfully curated selection of materials, decorative elements and goodies, and will be guided to create their own personalised Advent calendar / goodies bag containing 25 individual surprises—one for each day.

The workshop is designed as an interactive and festive experience where participants can explore their creativity, enjoy the studio atmosphere and take home something they have personally created.

3. Proposed partnership and commercial structure

To keep the first collaboration simple, transparent and mutually beneficial, we propose the following ticket structure:

Participant ticket price₹1,199
Aryka Studio – food & beverage allocation₹300 per confirmed participant
Sundarlysh Memories – workshop allocation₹899 per confirmed participant

A sum of ₹300 from every confirmed participant's ticket will be allocated exclusively to Aryka Studio towards the agreed food and beverage offering. This allocation is built into the ticket price, giving the studio a clear, assured amount per paid registration.

For this first workshop partnership, we suggest avoiding separate expense-sharing or additional cost-splitting arrangements. Sundarlysh Memories will take responsibility for the workshop concept, creative materials, activity planning and event coordination within its ₹899 allocation.

Any additional food or beverage orders placed by participants beyond the agreed workshop inclusions will belong entirely to Aryka Studio. These additional purchases will not be shared with or treated as revenue for Sundarlysh Memories. If a coupon or redemption code is used for the offering, it will be structured so that the studio's allocation remains clear and transparent.

4. Roles and collaboration

Sundarlysh Memories will:

  • Conceptualise and curate the Advent Calendar Creation Workshop.
  • Arrange the materials, decorative elements and goodies required for participants to create their 25-day Advent calendar.
  • Plan and facilitate the workshop activity, and coordinate the participant experience.
  • Create the workshop promotional communication and drive registrations through its own networks and channels.

Aryka Studio will:

  • Provide the agreed food and beverage offering, supported by the ₹300 allocation per confirmed participant.
  • Promote the workshop through its social media platforms, including posts, stories and reminders.
  • Display the workshop brochure or promotional material at the studio.
  • Share the workshop details with its customer community and relevant personal and business contacts.
  • Retain all revenue from additional food and beverage orders placed by attendees.

5. Shared benefits

  • A clear studio allocation of ₹300 for every confirmed participant, built into the ticket price.
  • Additional revenue from independent food and beverage purchases by attendees.
  • An opportunity to welcome new guests, increase footfall and introduce Aryka Studio to potential repeat customers.
  • Joint brand visibility through coordinated social media and in-studio promotion.
  • A distinctive festive workshop that can help both partners build awareness and explore future creative collaborations.

6. Moving forward together

We see this as a collaborative experience—not simply a workshop hosted at a venue. By combining our strengths, we can create an event that is enjoyable for participants and commercially worthwhile for both partners. The first edition will also give us an opportunity to understand audience response and build a foundation for more workshops and experiences together.

We look forward to discussing the agreed food and beverage inclusions, participant capacity, registration process and settlement timeline so that both teams can move ahead with clarity and confidence.
