A Christmas countdown: one small surprise to open every day in December until Christmas. At this workshop you create the whole calendar yourself.

Not at all. Everything is set up for you and our team guides you through each step.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes, the workshop is fun for all ages. Please book a seat for each participant.

What do I take home?

Your own handmade advent calendar: decorated gift bags or a goodies box filled with surprises you chose.
