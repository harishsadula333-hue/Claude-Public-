FAQGood to know
What's included in ₹999?

The 2-hour guided workshop, all 24 kinds of props and gifts, the gift bags or goodies box, and ₹100 of redeemable food & beverage at Stuff idly India. Everything you make goes home with you.

Do I need to bring anything?

Nothing at all. Just bring yourself (and your friends!). Everything is set up for you.

Can I gift a seat?

Yes! Book in your friend's name or message us on WhatsApp, and we'll send them an invite.

What is an advent calendar?

A Christmas countdown: one small surprise to open every day in December until Christmas. At this workshop you create the whole calendar yourself.

Do I need any craft experience?

Not at all. Everything is set up for you and our team guides you through each step.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes, the workshop is fun for all ages. Please book a seat for each participant.

What do I take home?

Your own handmade advent calendar: decorated gift bags or a goodies box filled with surprises you chose.

How do I get my ticket?

As soon as your payment goes through, your e-ticket appears on this page (you can download it as a PDF) and a copy is emailed to you. Please show it at entry, on your phone or printed. Questions? DM us on Instagram.

Is the payment secure?

Yes. Payments are handled by Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking, wallets). We never see your card or UPI details.
