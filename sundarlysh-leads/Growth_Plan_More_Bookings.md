## 2. Highest-impact actions (do these first)
1. **All bookings go through the landing page and DMs (no ticket apps).** Share the landing page with `?src=` tracking everywhere, and close in DMs with `DM_Sales_Playbook.md`. The new Facebook page can carry a Facebook Event that links to the landing page.
2. **Micro-influencer barter.** Offer 2–3 free seats to Hyderabad mom, lifestyle or food creators (5k–50k followers, living near Gachibowli/Kokapet) in exchange for a reel on the day **and** a story beforehand. One honest creator story usually beats ₹1,000 of ads.
3. **Instagram Collab with Van Lavino** on the launch post. Their followers already go to the venue.
4. **Show the real product.** Display one **finished advent calendar** at the Van Lavino counter next to the table tent (with QR). Post a 10–15 s real video of the props and the finished calendar; real footage converts better than AI images.
5. **Make the price feel like value:** "₹1,399 = workshop + all 24 props & gifts + ₹500 café voucher + you take everything home". Effectively about ₹899 for the workshop.
6. **Group offers** (needs the client's OK):
   - *Bring a friend:* 2 seats for ₹2,599 (save ₹199)
   - *Group of 4:* 10% off
   - *Parent + child:* the child shares the parent's kit for ₹699

   Groups fill seats faster than singles.
7. **Referral code:** every person who books gets a code. A friend who books with it gets ₹100 off, and the booker gets a free hot chocolate.
8. **Paid ad, done right:** ₹100/day on the best reel with the "Send WhatsApp message" button. Reply within 10 minutes; most sales are won in the chat.

- **Preschools and kids' centres near FD** (Safari Kid, KLAY, Little Otters, SKILLNOOK): ask them to forward the flyer to their parents' groups.
- **MommyFest, 21–22 Nov, Lake Shore Mall, Y Junction** (family festival, tickets from ₹249 on District). Ask the organisers (the Creativee Community runs it) whether you can hand out flyers, keep a small demo table, or run a 15-minute mini craft corner. It's a crowd of exactly the right audience, one week before the workshop.
- **Corporate:** offices around Financial District plan December team activities. Pitch the same workshop as a private team session (₹1,399 × team size) or a lunchtime version. One corporate booking can be worth 15–30 seats.

## 5. Converting interest into bookings
- Reply to every DM, comment and WhatsApp message within 1 hour, using the quick replies.
- Put the booking link in the Instagram bio, every story (link sticker) and the WhatsApp catalogue.
- Follow up "maybe" leads twice: the next day, and 3 days before the event.
- Give a reason to book now: the early bird (the first 5 bookings get 10% off any December workshop), and post true seat counts only.
- After booking, send the confirmation (message D) and ask: "Would you like to bring a friend? Here's ₹100 off for them."

## 6. Content that keeps interest going (until the event)
- **Day 1:** teaser (v3) + countdown story
- **Day 2:** main reel + Collab + "What's inside?" carousel (real props)
- **Every 2 days:** a story poll or quiz ("Gift bags or goodies box?" / "Guess the 24 surprises")
- **Final 3 days:** behind-the-scenes packing reel, guest DMs (with permission), "See you Sunday!"
- **Event day:** live stories, guest reactions. Film everything; this footage sells every future workshop.
