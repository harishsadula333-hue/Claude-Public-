# DM sales playbook: turn every DM into a booking (Instagram + WhatsApp)

**The rule:** reply within **10 minutes**, give the booking link in the **first** reply, and always end with a question. Most people decide in the first 5 minutes of a chat.

## 1. First reply (copy-paste, save it as a quick reply)
> Hi {name}! 🎄 Thank you for your interest!
> *Christmas Advent Calendar Workshop*
> 📅 Sun 29 Nov · 5–7 PM · 📍 Van Lavino Café & Patisserie, Financial District
> 🎁 You make your own advent calendar with 24 surprises and take it home
> 🎟️ ₹1,399, which includes all props & gifts **+ a ₹500 food voucher** at the café
>
> Book here (30 sec): {landing page link}?src=dm
> How many seats should I block for you? 😊

## 2. Close fast
- **"How many seats should I block?"** Assume the sale and ask for a number, not "are you interested?".
- When they say a number: *"Done ✅ I've reserved {n} seat(s) for you for 24 hours. Here's the payment link: {link}. Once paid, I'll send your confirmation."*
- Holding a seat for **24 hours** creates real urgency without lying.

## 3. Common questions: ready answers
| They ask | You reply |
|---|---|
| "It's expensive" | "Totally get it! ₹500 of it comes back as food at Van Lavino, so the workshop with all 24 props & gifts is effectively ₹899, and you take the whole calendar home 🎁" |
| "Can I bring my kid?" | "Yes! Kids love it. Each child who makes their own calendar needs a seat, or they can share yours and help you 😊" |
| "I'll think about it" | "Sure! Shall I hold a seat for you till tomorrow evening so you don't miss out? There's no payment until you confirm." |
| "Do I need experience?" | "Not at all. Everything is set up, and we guide you step by step. It's relaxing and fun ✨" |
| "Can I come with friends?" | "Yes please! It's even more fun in a group. How many of you? I'll keep the seats together 🎄" |
| "Is it really the first in Hyderabad?" | "Yes, it's the first advent-calendar-making workshop in Hyderabad, and the first time we're doing it! 🎉" |
| "What's an advent calendar?" | "A Christmas countdown: 24 little surprises, one to open every day till Christmas. You make and fill all of them yourself!" |
| "Where exactly?" | "Van Lavino Café & Patisserie, Financial District. I'll send the Google Maps pin with your confirmation 📍" |

## 4. Follow-ups (people who asked but didn't book)
- **Next day:** "Hi {name}! Just checking, should I keep a seat for you for Sunday's advent calendar workshop? 🎄"
- **3 days before:** "Hi {name}! We're almost full for Sunday 🎁 Would you like me to block a seat for you?" *(only if that's true)*
- **The day before:** "Last call! Tomorrow 5 PM at Van Lavino 🎄 Reply YES and I'll send you the link."
- Then stop. Three touches maximum; never spam.

## 5. After someone books (each booker brings one more)
- Confirmation: *"Yay, you're in! 🎉 Sun 29 Nov, 5–7 PM, Van Lavino Café & Patisserie, Financial District. Bring a friend? They get ₹100 off with your name 💛"*
- Ask for a **story share**: "Would you share our post on your story? We'll repost you 🎄"

## 6. Get more DMs in the first place
- **Story with a question sticker:** "Want to make your own advent calendar? 🎄" Everyone who replies is a warm lead; DM them the first reply.
- **Comment keyword:** at the end of the reel caption, add *Comment "XMAS" and we'll DM you the details*. Reply to every comment with a DM within the hour. (Optional: ManyChat's free plan can send the DM automatically.)
- **Personal network first (fastest bookings):** the founder and every team member personally message 20–30 friends, colleagues and relatives one by one (not as a broadcast), with the teaser plus the first reply. Warm contacts book 5–10× more than strangers.
- **Instagram "Message" button ads:** ₹100/day; every ad click opens a DM, which you answer with the first reply.
- **Pin the reel** to the top of the profile, and put the landing page in the bio as `{link}?src=ig-bio`.
