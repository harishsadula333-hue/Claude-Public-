## 2. Call / DM script (for the role-play)
Goal: answer warmly, explain clearly, collect details, close the booking. **Don't use an AI voice agent.** Prachi wants a real person.

1. **Greet**: "Hi, thank you for calling Sundarlysh Memories, this is Harish. How can I help you?"
2. **Find their interest**: "Are you calling about our Christmas Advent Calendar Workshop?"
3. **Explain in 3 lines**:
   - "It's Hyderabad's first advent calendar workshop, on Sunday 29 November, 5 to 7 PM, at Van Lavino Café & Patisserie in the Financial District."
   - "We set up 24 kinds of props and little gifts, and you create and decorate your own 24 surprise bags or a goodies box. No pre-packed gifts, it's all made by you."
   - "You take it home and open one surprise every day till Christmas. It's great for friends, families and kids."
4. **Price**: "It's ₹1,400 per person, and that includes a ₹500 food voucher you can use at the café." *(Confirm with Prachi before quoting.)*
5. **Early bird**: "The first 5 bookings also get 10% off any of our December workshops."
6. **Collect**: name, WhatsApp number, number of seats, kids yes/no. Add them to the Google Sheet.
7. **Close**: "Shall I reserve your seats? I'll send the booking link on WhatsApp now."
8. **If they're unsure**: "No problem! Can I send you the details on WhatsApp so you can check with your friends?" Mark them *Follow up*.
9. **If you can't answer**: "Let me check that with our team and call you back within the hour." Note the question for Prachi. Never guess.
10. **After hours**: the business phone is switched off after working hours. Reply to messages the next morning.

Common questions:
- *Do I need craft skills?* No, everything is set up and we guide you.
- *Can kids join?* Yes, book a seat per participant.
- *What do I take home?* Your own handmade advent calendar with 24 surprises.
- *Parking/location?* Share the café's Google Maps link privately on WhatsApp. **Never post our own address publicly.**
