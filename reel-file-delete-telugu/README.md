## How it is made

Everything is generated by code, so there are no third-party media or licensing issues:

- **Voice:** Microsoft neural Telugu male voice `te-IN-MohanNeural` (via `edge-tts`), generated one sentence at a time with tight pauses and word-level timestamps.
- **Animation:** `src/reel.html` + `src/anim.js`. Each frame is a deterministic function of time, keyed to the word timestamps. Includes a lip-synced cartoon presenter (blinking, gestures, expressions), karaoke-style Telugu captions and six illustrated scenes.
- **Music and SFX:** `tools/audio.py` synthesizes an original 110 BPM track and about 30 sound effects (whooshes, pops, stamp, eraser, scanner, bells…), with the music ducked under the voice.
- **Render:** `tools/render.js` captures frames with Playwright/Chromium and encodes them with ffmpeg.

Rebuild: `tools/make.sh` (needs python3 with numpy, scipy and edge-tts; node with playwright; ffmpeg).
Edit the words in `src/script.txt` (`spoken|shown` tokens). If the word count changes, update the word indices used in `anim.js`.
