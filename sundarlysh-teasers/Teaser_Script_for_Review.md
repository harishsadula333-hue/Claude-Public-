## Changes made after the 19 Nov meeting
- **Cake painting picture removed.** A plain white Christmas cake with berries and holly is used instead. Other-workshop photos (canvas painting, pipe-cleaner trees) were also removed, so only advent and Christmas photos remain.
- **No black background.** The colours are Christmas red, green and white, with snow and confetti.
- **Distinct fonts:** *Advent Calendar* in an elegant script, the venue in italic, and the date in a classic serif.
- **"Magical" replaced by "wonderful"** in this video, the gold teaser, the reel cover, the countdown story, the captions and the calendar.
- **Less like the sample videos.** Outline echo text, glitch effects, typewriter text and "stay tuned" tapes were replaced with festive effects (confetti, snow, bouncing letters, Christmas ribbons).
