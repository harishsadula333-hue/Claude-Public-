13 · Maison S·A

A fashion-house monogram. A Bodoni S just touches the star-A, and a tiny gold heart replaces the crossbar. The name is set in widely spaced Bodoni capitals.

14 · Arch

One fine gold pointed arch stands for the A, with the star on its tip and a slender S inside. The airy Italiana name gives it a quiet, boutique feel.

15 · Ring

The star-A alone in a fine gold ring, like a jeweller's hallmark, with the star breaking through the top. It works as a clean, timeless seal.
