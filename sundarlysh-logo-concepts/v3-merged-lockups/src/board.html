10 · Crowned A

The A inside SUNDAR grows into the emblem, and its head becomes a crown. Its thin leg runs on into the thread under SUND, and its crossbar flows over the R into "lysh".

DARK VERSION

11 · Jhumka A

The A inside the word becomes a jhumka earring: a star stud on top, a heart gem on the crossbar, and pearls hanging from the baseline. The leg and crossbar flow on into the words.

LIGHT VERSION

12 · Heart Thread

The brand's thread becomes one big heart holding the whole name: wine for "Sundar", gold for "lysh". The star sits in the heart's dip, and the heart weaves behind the S and under the thread.
