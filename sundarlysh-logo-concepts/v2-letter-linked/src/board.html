SUNDARLYSH

EMBLEMS BUILT FROM OUR OWN LETTERS · WITH THE ORIGINAL WORDMARK

06 · Crown of A

Three star-A's from the wordmark rise into a crown, the way WDC turned its W into a crown, but drawn from our own letter. Pearl swags link the A's, and the heart is the crown jewel.

DARK VERSION

07 · Jhumka A

The star-A becomes an Indian jhumka earring: the star is the stud, the A is the bell, the heart is the gem on the crossbar, and pearls hang along the rim.

LIGHT VERSION

08 · S·A Monogram

The wordmark's own S, woven through the star-A. The A's thin leg flows on into the golden thread, just as it does in the logo.

DARK VERSION

09 · Heart of Two S

The S of SUNDAR meets its reflection, wine for "Sundar" and gold for "lysh", and their tops join into a heart.
